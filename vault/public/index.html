Change the account password

The account password signs you in here and in the desktop app. Changing it signs out every other device and browser; this one stays signed in. Your vault master password is not changed: it was never on the server.

12–128 characters, used exactly as typed.

Two-factor authentication

A code from an authenticator app (TOTP) in addition to your account password when you sign in. It protects the account sign-in only; the vault master password is a separate thing and stays as it is.

Add this secret to your authenticator app: on a phone, tap Open in an authenticator app; elsewhere, type or paste the secret (spaces do not matter) or the full link. Then enter the code the app shows to confirm. The secret is shown once and is not kept by this page.

Passkeys

        
A passkey signs you in to this web vault with your device's screen lock, a password manager or a security key: as the second step after your account password, or on its own without email and password. Passkeys come on top of two-factor authentication: they need it to be on, it cannot be turned off while you have passkeys, and the desktop app keeps signing in with your authenticator code. A passkey signs in to the account only; opening the vault still needs your vault master password.
