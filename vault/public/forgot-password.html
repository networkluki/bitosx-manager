Forgot your account password?

Enter the email address of your account and we send a link to choose a new account password. This resets only the password you sign in with. It cannot recover your vault master password: the vault stays encrypted with it, and BitosX never has it.

The link in the email is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once. Every signed-in device is signed out when the password changes.
